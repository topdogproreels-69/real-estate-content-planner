Questions

What exactly is included?

The 40-page 30-Day Real Estate Social Media Content Planner PDF, a short guide to the templates, and 10 editable 1080×1080 PowerPoint templates (a combined file and individual files), plus fonts, PNG previews, and a read-me.

Do I need Canva Pro or a paid PowerPoint subscription?

No. The templates are .pptx files. A regular PowerPoint install, a free Google account with Google Slides, or a free Canva account (File > Import) is enough. Google Slides and Canva already include the fonts; install the included fonts if you use PowerPoint.

Are these Instagram templates for Reels, carousels, and Stories too?

The 10 graphic templates are square 1080×1080 feed posts (new listing, just sold, open house, tip cards, and similar). The 30-day planner also gives you hooks, captions, and visual ideas for Reels, carousels, and Stories. You would film or build those from the visual ideas, not from a separate video file.

Can I change the photos, colors, and text?

Yes. Every text line is editable, including the [PLACEHOLDER] fields. Swap photos in PowerPoint, Google Slides, or Canva. Use your own listing photos, and only post client reviews with written permission.

Does this include market statistics or client testimonials I can post as-is?

No, and that is intentional. You add your own local numbers from your MLS (and cite the source) and only real, approved client quotes. The Market Update and Client Testimonial templates are blank structures for those facts.

Is this only for Instagram?

It is written as an Instagram content system — captions, hashtags, Reels, Stories, and square posts. The same captions and square graphics can be adapted for Facebook or LinkedIn if that is where your clients are.

Will this work for my city and brokerage?

Yes, if you replace every bracket with your details and add any required brokerage name, license number, or disclosure. The planner reminds you to follow your commission rules, brokerage policy, fair housing laws, and Instagram’s terms. It is marketing content, not legal advice.

Yes. Week 1 starts with a simple introduction, buyer education, and a local community post, so you can show up even before you have a long list of closings. Skip or save any listing-specific day until you have a real listing you have permission to share.
